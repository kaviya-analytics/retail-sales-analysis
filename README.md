## retail-sales-analysis
## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, top-performing product categories, payment methods, store types, and cities. An interactive dashboard was created to present key performance indicators and business insights in a simple and visual format.
## Objectives
- Analyze overall retail sales performance
- Identify top-performing product categories and cities
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Compare sales performance across store types
- Track sales trends over time
- Generate actionable business insights
## Dataset
The dataset contains 50 retail transactions with the following fields:
- Bill ID
- Customer Name
- City
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Total Amount
- Payment Method
- Store Type
- Visit Date
## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas
- Data Visualization
## Dashboard Features
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Sales by Payment Method
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Store Type
- Sales by City
- Sales Trend
- Interactive Slicers
## Key Business Insights
1. Electronics is the major revenue driver, generating ₹2,96,000 (77.6% of total sales).
2. Mall Stores contribute ₹3,59,800 (94.4% of total sales), making them the strongest sales channel.
3. Credit Card transactions account for ₹2,96,000 (77.6% of total sales).
4. Grocery has the highest order volume with 18 orders and 108 units sold.
5. Electronics generates much higher revenue per order than Grocery, showing the impact of product value on revenue.
6. High-value Electronics transactions are concentrated in a few cities, creating opportunities for targeted marketing. 
## Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-value Electronics products to maximize revenue.
- Strengthen sales strategies in Mall Stores.
- Use targeted marketing for cities with high-value Electronics purchases.
- Introduce cross-selling and promotional offers for Grocery and other high-volume categories.
- Monitor payment preferences to improve customer convenience.
- ## Project Outcome
- The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of retail sales performance and helps identify important patterns that can support data-driven business decisions.
